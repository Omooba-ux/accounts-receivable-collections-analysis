# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Overview

This section summarises the key findings generated from the Accounts Receivable & Collections Analytics project.

The analysis focused on understanding payment behaviour, outstanding receivables, ageing risk, and customer exposure to support better collection decisions.

---

# Key Findings

## 1. Receivables Position

The analysis reviewed invoice transactions with a total invoice value of:

**$15.54M**

The analysis identified:

- Outstanding receivables of **$1.56M**
- Seriously late exposure of **$2.32M**

These figures highlight the importance of monitoring customer payment behaviour and improving collection visibility.

---

# 2. Accounts Receivable Ageing

Ageing analysis was performed to understand how long outstanding balances remained unpaid.

Key observations:

- Older receivables represented increased collection risk.
- 90+ day balances required closer monitoring due to the potential impact on cash flow.
- Ageing segmentation helped prioritise collection activities.

---

# 3. Customer Exposure Analysis

Customer-level analysis was conducted to identify where receivables risk was concentrated.

Insights included:

- Certain customers contributed a larger proportion of outstanding balances.
- Customer segmentation helped identify exposure across SME, Mid-Size, and Enterprise customers.
- Industry-level analysis highlighted areas with higher receivable concentration.

---

# 4. Payment Performance

Payment behaviour analysis reviewed invoice settlement patterns.

The analysis examined:

- On-time payments
- Late payments
- Seriously late payments
- Payment delays by customer segment

Understanding these patterns provides visibility into collection effectiveness and customer payment behaviour.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the findings:

## 1. Prioritise Collection Activities

Focus collection efforts on:

- Older ageing balances
- High-value outstanding invoices
- Customers with repeated late payment patterns

---

## 2. Monitor High-Risk Customers

Establish regular review of customers with:

- Significant outstanding balances
- Consistent payment delays
- High exposure concentration

---

## 3. Improve Receivables Reporting

Maintain regular reporting dashboards to track:

- Outstanding balances
- Ageing movement
- Collection progress
- Customer payment trends

---

## 4. Use Data-Driven Collection Strategies

Customer segmentation and payment behaviour analysis can support more targeted collection approaches instead of applying the same strategy across all customers.

---

# Business Value

The analysis provides finance teams with improved visibility into receivables performance and supports better decision-making around collections, cash flow management, and customer risk monitoring.
